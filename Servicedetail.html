How do I book this service?

You can use the Book This Service button on this page to visit our booking page and submit your request.

Do I need to check garment care labels?

Yes. Care labels provide useful information about fabric composition and recommended handling. Please share any special care instructions when booking.

Can I request special garment care?

Yes. Mention delicate fabrics, specific stains or other special requirements when placing your order so they can be considered.

How long does the service take?

Turnaround time depends on the service, garment requirements and order volume. Please confirm the expected completion time when booking.

Is pickup and delivery available?

Pickup and delivery availability depends on your service area. Please check the available options while scheduling your order.
